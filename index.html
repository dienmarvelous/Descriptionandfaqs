Frequently asked questions

Yes — all orders over $99 AUD qualify for free standard shipping Australia-wide. Express and same-day delivery options are also available at checkout for most metro areas.

All devices and accessories sold through TechGear Pro come with a minimum 1-year manufacturer's warranty. Many of our premium brands — including Sony, Logitech, and Samsung — offer up to 2 years. Warranty details are listed on each product page.
Can I return a product if it doesn't meet my expectations? +
Absolutely. We offer free returns within 30 days of purchase for all unopened or defective items. Simply contact our support team and we'll arrange a pickup or drop-off return at no cost to you.
Are your products compatible with both Mac and Windows? +
Most of the accessories we stock — including keyboards, mice, headphones, and monitors — are cross-platform compatible. Compatibility details are clearly listed on each product page. If you're unsure, our team is happy to help you find the right match.
Do you offer bulk or business pricing? +
Yes — we offer special pricing for businesses, schools, and teams ordering 5 or more units. Contact our corporate team for a custom quote and we'll tailor a package to your budget and requirements.
Are online prices different from in-store prices? +
Yes — our online exclusive prices are lower than what you'll find at most retail locations. We're able to pass on the savings because we operate primarily online, which means better value for you on every order.
How do I know which accessories are compatible with my device? +
Every product page lists full compatibility details including supported operating systems, connector types, and device requirements. You can also use our search filter to shop by brand or device type, or reach out to our team for personalised recommendations.
